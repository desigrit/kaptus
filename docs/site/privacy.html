Privacy, in plain words.

Kaptus Android privacy policy. Updated November 4, 2026.

Your microphone audio stays on your phone. Kaptus processes it in memory to find your place in existing captions.

This policy describes the Kaptus Android app maintained by desigrit.

Microphone and speech recognition

With your permission, Kaptus captures nearby dialogue when you open captions with an available auto-seek combination or tap Resync. Recognition runs on your device. Audio is held only in memory, is never written to an audio file and is never sent to Kaptus, OpenSubtitles or a speech-recognition service.

The microphone stops when a scene is found, the attempt times out, you cancel, or the app leaves the foreground. A running caption clock can continue while you use another app. Manual caption timing does not require microphone permission. You can revoke that permission in Android Settings.

Captions and settings on your device

Kaptus stores downloaded or imported captions, title and episode metadata, language choices, caption timing mappings, History and display preferences in app-private storage. Your document picker grants access to SRT files you choose. Kaptus does not scan your photo or media library.

Your OpenSubtitles API key and any optional username and password are encrypted with a key protected by Android Keystore. App data is excluded from Android backup and device transfer.

When Kaptus connects to the internet

  • OpenSubtitles: Searching or downloading sends your search text, selected title or episode, caption language, requested subtitle identifiers and API key to OpenSubtitles over HTTPS. If you provide optional account credentials, they are sent to OpenSubtitles to sign in. Kaptus uses the returned session token for authenticated requests. The provider and its download infrastructure receive the requests and your connection's IP address.
  • Speech model download: When you choose to download the optional multilingual model, Kaptus contacts Hugging Face and its delivery infrastructure over HTTPS. They receive the model request and your connection's IP address. The downloaded model is verified before use. No microphone audio or OpenSubtitles credentials are sent with this request.
  • Links you open: Privacy, help and account links open in your browser. Those sites receive ordinary browser requests and apply their own privacy policies.

OpenSubtitles manages its accounts, request records and download allowances. You can review account and privacy options at OpenSubtitles. Model hosting is covered by the Hugging Face privacy policy. This policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages under the GitHub privacy statement.

Kaptus has no developer-operated server for collecting app activity, no advertising SDK and no analytics or automatic crash-reporting SDK. It does not sell personal data.

Retention and deletion

Saved captions, History and settings stay on your device until app data is cleared or Kaptus is uninstalled. Remove saved provider credentials by clearing the API key in Kaptus Settings and saving the change. You can delete the optional speech model in Settings.

To remove all local Kaptus data, use Android Settings, Apps, Kaptus, Storage, Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Original SRT files in your own local or cloud storage remain under your control. Data already sent to OpenSubtitles or hosting providers is governed by those providers' retention and deletion processes. Kaptus does not create a separate Kaptus account.
